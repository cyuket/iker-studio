# cut: full (youtube-landscape, 60-150s)

## hook (PROBLEM) - The situation

A client wants Saturday at eleven. Without a booking link, that is a long chat, your account number sent by DM, and a transfer screenshot you have to find later. Here is what your client sees instead.

## booking-link (DEMO) - The booking link

> The public booking page from the local demo seed. Point at the services list.

It starts with your booking link, the one in your Instagram bio or WhatsApp status. There is no app to download and no account to create. The client sees your services with the name, price and duration of each. This demo uses made-up data.

## choose-time (DEMO) - Choosing a time

> The client adds a service, picks a day and the 11:00 am slot, and types their details.

They add a service and pick a day. Only times that are really open show up, so a slot you already booked is not offered. Then they enter their name and phone number.

## policy (EXPLANATION) - The Refund Policy

> Motion graphic, not product UI: the policy appears before the payment step.

Before they pay, they read your Refund Policy and tick to accept it. The booking keeps a copy of the exact text they agreed to. You set policies in Settings, depending on your plan.

## pay-proof (DEMO) - Paying the deposit

> Hold on the Pay & confirm step: the deposit, the bank details, Upload proof of payment.

Now the payment step. They see the deposit you set for the service and your bank details. They transfer the money to your own account, outside the app, and upload proof of payment. The booking cannot be submitted without it. Then they get a page that says their proof was received.

## chapter-owner (EXPLANATION) - What you see

> Silent chapter card.

## owner-confirm (DEMO) - What you see

> Dashboard, Review payment, the booking with View proof, Confirm booking and Reject payment.

On your side, the booking shows under Awaiting confirmation. Open it and view the proof, then check your bank account. Iker does not check the transfer for you. If the money is there, choose Confirm booking. If it is not, choose Reject payment.

## outcome (OUTCOME) - What changes

Nothing counts as income until you confirm it. The proof of payment sits on the booking, next to the policy they accepted. And Iker takes no commission on bookings or deposits.
