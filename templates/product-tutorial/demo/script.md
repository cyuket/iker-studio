## intro (INTRO) - Title

This is how to record a client's deposit on a booking in Iker.

## problem (PROBLEM) - Why this matters

A deposit that only lives in a chat is easy to lose track of, and so is the balance still owed.

## steps (EXPLANATION) - The steps

There are three steps. Add the booking, record the payment, and check the balance.

## chapter-setup (EXPLANATION) - Chapter card

> Silent chapter card.

## setup (DEMO) - Set it up

> Recorded against the synthetic demo fixture.

Open Bookings and choose Add walk-in. Pick the service and the time, then enter the client's phone number and name. This demo uses made-up data.

## result (DEMO) - See the result

On the booking, choose Record a payment, enter the amount received, and save. The booking now shows what is paid so far and the balance remaining.

## recap (OUTCOME) - Recap

That is all it takes. Each payment is saved with its own date, so a part payment today and the rest next week add up on the same booking.
