# cut: full (youtube-landscape, 30-180s)

## hook (PROBLEM) - The situation

A client says she has sent the deposit. The screenshot is somewhere in a WhatsApp chat, between a voice note and a price list.

## gap (EXPLANATION) - Why it breaks

A chat is good for talking. It is not a record. It cannot tell you who has paid, how much, or what is still owed.

## how-it-works (DIAGRAM) - A better way

Keep the payment with the booking. The client transfers to your account, uploads proof, and you confirm it.

## product-intro (DEMO) - In Iker

> Synthetic demo footage.

In Iker you work from your dashboard, and payments waiting for your confirmation show up there. This demo uses made-up data.

## booking (DEMO) - On the booking

Each booking shows its price, what has been paid so far, and the balance remaining.

## payment (DEMO) - Recording a payment

When the client pays the rest, you record it on the same booking, and the balance updates.

## confirm (EXPLANATION) - You stay in charge

Nothing counts as income until you confirm it. Confirmed payments stay as they are, and a mistake is corrected with a new entry.

## outcome (OUTCOME) - What changes

Who has paid now has one answer, on the booking, and your monthly report is built from payments you confirmed.
