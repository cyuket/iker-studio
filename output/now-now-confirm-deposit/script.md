## intro (INTRO) - Now now

> character-scene. Chioma, phone in hand, cheerful, shrugging. Speech bubble: "I'll send it now now." Fictional client Chioma O. Labelled Illustration. [SFX: message pop]

Chioma sends a message. I'll send it now now. It is Monday. Now now. The two most dangerous words in booking.

## problem (PROBLEM) - Amaka's week

> timeline, labelled "Amaka's week (illustration)". Day 1 Monday "now now". Day 2 Tuesday reminder. Day 3 Wednesday reminder. Day 4 Thursday "abeg I forgot". Saturday 10:30 stays blocked the whole way across. The reminders are Amaka's own messages, not Iker's. [SFX: phone alarm repeating, cut on "forgot"] [GIF]

Amaka is a makeup artist in Abuja. Chioma wants bridal makeup on Saturday at ten thirty. Forty-five thousand naira, with a fifteen thousand naira deposit. Amaka keeps the date for her. Tuesday, a reminder. Wednesday, another one. Thursday, abeg I forgot. Four days. Two reminders. One abeg I forgot. And a Saturday slot nobody else could take.

## maybe-booked (EXPLANATION) - Maybe or booked

> comparison. Left: a date with no deposit behind it, "maybe". Right: a date with proof behind it, "booked".

Here is the difference. A date with no deposit is a maybe. A date with proof is a booking. Proof of payment does not make anyone pay faster. It gives you something real to check before you confirm the slot.

## money-path (EXPLANATION) - Where the money goes

> pipeline. A payment object moves: client, bank transfer to Amaka, proof uploaded, "Awaiting confirmation". Label: "The money goes to you, not through Iker."

In Iker, your client pays you, the way they already do, by bank transfer to your own account. Then they upload proof. The booking shows Awaiting confirmation, and the slot is held while you check. Iker does not touch the money. It keeps track.

## check-proof (DEMO) - Check the proof

> product, iPhone frame. iker-amaka-chioma-proof-phone: the booking shows "Awaiting confirmation". Spotlight on the payment's proof (View proof). The proof image itself cannot be recorded (it opens in a new tab): any proof image shown is a labelled mock. Demo data.

Here is a booking waiting on a phone. The business and client on screen are demo data. You see the booking, the amount and the proof. Check it against your bank app.

## decide (DEMO) - You decide

> decision, labelled Illustration. Matches your account: tap Confirm booking. Does not match: tap Reject payment. [SFX: confirm chime on Confirm]

Matches your account? Tap Confirm booking. Does not match? Tap Reject payment. Iker does not check your bank for you. You decide.

## result (DEMO) - Written down

> product, iPhone frame. After confirming: Paid so far ₦15,000, Balance remaining ₦30,000. Calm check mark, no confetti. Demo data.

After you confirm, the deposit counts as paid. Paid so far, fifteen thousand naira. Balance remaining, thirty thousand naira. Written down on the booking, not buried in a chat.
