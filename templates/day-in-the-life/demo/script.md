# cut: full (youtube-landscape, 90-180s)

## hook (PROBLEM) - Meet the owner

> Character scene. Kemi is labelled "(fictional)" on screen. Phone in hand, a thought bubble with the question.

Meet Kemi. She runs a small hair salon, and she is made up for this video. Her day starts on her phone: who is coming in today, who has paid a deposit, and who still owes? Most days, that answer lives in WhatsApp chats and her memory.

## the-day (EXPLANATION) - The day ahead

> Timeline of the day. Each event is one of the scenes that follow.

Let us follow her through one day. Bookings in the morning, a walk-in before lunch, a deposit paid by transfer in the afternoon, and the balance at closing time.

## morning (DEMO) - Morning

> Synthetic demo footage of the dashboard. Point at the Awaiting confirmation card.

Kemi starts on her dashboard. Her clients booked from her booking link, with no app to download. Any payment she still needs to check shows up under Awaiting confirmation. This demo uses made-up data.

## walk-in (DEMO) - A walk-in

> Synthetic demo footage of Add walk-in.

Just before lunch, Tola walks in without a booking. Kemi opens Bookings and chooses Add walk-in. She picks the service and the time, enters Tola's phone number and name, and the amount received. The walk-in takes the slot like any other booking, so that time no longer shows as open on her booking link.

## deposit (EXPLANATION) - A deposit by transfer

> Character scene. Bisi (fictional client) sends money to Kemi's own bank account, then a proof of payment tile lands on the booking. No product screen is drawn.

In the afternoon, Bisi books a braiding appointment from Kemi's link. She transfers a fifteen thousand naira deposit straight to Kemi's own bank account, then uploads her proof of payment with the booking. Iker never holds the money. It keeps the proof on the booking, where Kemi can find it.

## confirm (EXPLANATION) - Confirming it

> Steps: check the proof, Confirm booking or Reject payment, counted as income.

Between clients, Kemi opens Bisi's booking and checks the proof against her own bank app. If the money is there, she taps Confirm booking. If it is not, she taps Reject payment. Nothing counts as income until she confirms it.

## balance (DEMO) - The balance

> Synthetic demo footage of Record a payment.

In the evening, Bisi pays the remaining twenty-five thousand naira. Kemi chooses Record a payment on the same booking, enters the amount received, and saves. The booking now shows what is paid so far and the balance remaining.

## end-of-day (OUTCOME) - End of the day

> Story in outcome mode: one line per thing Kemi now knows, plus the limit about reminders.

At closing time, Kemi does not scroll back through chats to work out who paid. Each booking shows its own balance, and her monthly report is built from the payments she confirmed. Iker does not send reminders for her, so following up is still her call. And Iker takes no commission on her deposits.
