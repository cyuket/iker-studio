# Photos

Photos for "photo" scenes, used next to the existing illustrations. The photo files are not
committed to Git; only this catalogue is. Files named `own-*` are the owner's own photos and
are preferred whenever one fits the scene.

The stock photos below come from Pexels and Unsplash and are used under the Pexels License
(https://www.pexels.com/license/) or the Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license). Both
allow free commercial use with no attribution required. Neither allows selling a photo
unaltered (for example as a print or in a stock collection) or using it to imply that the
people shown, the photographer or the platform endorse Iker. The people in these photos are
models: never present them as Iker customers or as people who use Iker. Recheck the licence on
the source page before publishing a video that uses a photo.

All stock photos were downloaded on 2026-11-20, resized to at most 2400 px on the long edge and
saved as JPEG (quality 85).

Use on a scene in storyboard.json: `"visual": { "kind": "photo", "file": "salon-braiding-client.jpg", "alt": "<what it shows>" }`

## Adding a photo

When no photo here fits a scene, download one. Anyone (or `/iker`) may add photos this way:

1. Search Pexels (pexels.com) or Unsplash (unsplash.com) only. Skip Unsplash+ photos and the
   iStock/Getty "sponsored" results these sites show. Prefer Nigerian or West African people
   and settings, ordinary and warm, not glamour shoots.
2. Download into an empty temporary folder first, then look at the photo. Reject it if it shows
   a readable brand logo or shop sign, a readable phone or laptop screen, readable personal
   data, a child as the main subject, or anything sexual.
3. Resize to at most 2400 px on the long edge as JPEG quality 85
   (`sips -Z 2400 -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 85 in.jpg --out out.jpg`).
4. Name it in lowercase words joined by hyphens, ending `.jpg`, saying what it shows.
5. Add a row to the right table below: file, what it shows, orientation, size, the photo
   page's URL (not the image URL), the photographer and the licence. A photo without a
   confirmed page, photographer and licence is not kept.
