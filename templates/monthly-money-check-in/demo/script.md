# cut: full (youtube-landscape, 90-180s)

## hook (PROBLEM) - Month end

It is the last day of the month. Some clients paid by transfer, a walk-in paid cash, and what you spent on products is in a notebook. So, did the business actually make money this month?

## basics (EXPLANATION) - Three numbers

A month check-in starts with three numbers. What came in, what went out, and what was left. Everything else is detail.

## example (DIAGRAM) - A made-up month

> Data visual with made-up figures, labelled illustrative on screen.

Here is a made-up month for an example salon. 400,000 naira came in. 250,000 naira went out. So 150,000 naira was left. These are example figures, not real data.

## chapter-report (EXPLANATION) - Chapter card

> Silent chapter card.

## report (DEMO) - The Business report

> Recorded from iker-business-report against demo data. Highlight Money in, then Money out, then Profit.

In Iker, open Report under Accounting. For the month, the Business report shows Money in, Money out, and Profit. Money in is your confirmed payments and extra income, less refunds. This demo uses made-up data.

## pending (EXPLANATION) - Confirm before you read

A payment that is still waiting for you to confirm is not counted as income yet. So confirm what has really arrived before you read the month.

## transactions (DEMO) - The statement of accounts

> Recorded from iker-transactions against demo data. Point at the All tab title.

For the detail, open Transactions. It puts payments, refunds and expenses in one list, newest first. On the All tab, that list is called the statement of accounts.

## decide (EXPLANATION) - One thing to decide

Now decide one thing. Expenses you record, depending on your plan, are split by category under Where the money went. Find the biggest cost, ask whether it earned its place, and choose one change for next month.

## outcome (OUTCOME) - What you have now

So at month end you know what came in, what went out, and what was left, built from payments you confirmed. And you have one decision for next month.
