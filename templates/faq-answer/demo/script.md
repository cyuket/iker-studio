## question (PROBLEM) - The question

> Put the question on screen as the headline.

A client books from your booking link, sends the deposit to your account, and uploads proof of payment. Then she messages you to say she has paid. So what happens next?

## answer (EXPLANATION) - The short answer

The short answer: the booking waits for you to check it. Nothing counts as income until you confirm it. Iker does not check the transfer for you. The money goes to your account, so you are the one who knows it arrived.

## detail (DIAGRAM) - How it works

> Decision graphic: proof and your bank account go into your check, which ends in Confirm booking or Reject payment.

Here is how it works. A client has to upload proof before the booking can be sent, so the proof arrives with the booking. You check that the money is in your account. Then you choose Confirm booking, or Reject payment if it is not there. Meanwhile, the client sees a status page that says the proof was received.

## in-iker (DEMO) - Where to find it

> Synthetic demo footage: the Dashboard.

In Iker you start from your dashboard, and payments waiting for your confirmation show up there. This demo uses made-up data.

## on-the-booking (DEMO) - A closer look

> Synthetic demo footage: a booking detail page.

Open the booking and the booking shows its price, what is paid so far, and the balance remaining. When the client pays the rest, you record it on the same booking with Record a payment.

## next-step (OUTCOME) - What to do next

So the next time a client says she has paid, skip the chat scroll. Check your account, open the booking, and confirm or reject. A confirmed payment stays as it is, and a mistake is corrected with a new entry.

## cta (CTA) - Next step

To set up your booking link with the Iker team, get started at useiker.com.
