## compare (EXPLANATION) — A better way

> comparison. The usual way: the price typed again in every chat. A better way: one link with the price already on it. [SFX: reveal]

The price only lives inside each chat, so every client has to ask. The usual way, you type it again in every chat. A better way, one link with the price already on it. What if the price was already there before they asked?

In Iker, you set it up once. On the Rate card, each service gets its price, its duration and its deposit.

## packages (DEMO) — Packages

> product, iPad frame. iker-packages: services grouped into packages. Demo data.

## hours (DEMO) — Availability

> product, iPad frame. iker-availability: Weekly hours, then Blocked dates. Demo data.

And on Availability, you set your weekly hours and block the days you are away.

## link (DEMO) — What clients see

> product, iPad frame. iker-amaka-client-books-tablet: booking page with the rate card and price, open times, then Pay & confirm with the policy. Zoom on the price, then on the policy line.

Clients only see your link. Every service shows its price, and only times you are really open can be picked. Before they pay, they read your policy and tick to accept it. All of this is there before the first message. The business on screen is made-up demo data.

## decide (DEMO) — You confirm

> decision, motion graphic, labelled Illustration. Proof uploaded, then two branches: Confirm booking, or Reject payment. [SFX: confirm chime]

They pay the deposit by bank transfer, straight to your account, and upload their proof. Iker records it. It does not take the money. You look at the proof. If it is right, tap Confirm booking, and the slot is booked. If not, tap Reject payment.
