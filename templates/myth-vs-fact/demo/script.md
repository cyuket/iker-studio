## myth (PROBLEM) - The myth

Here is a worry we hear about booking apps. If clients book through Iker, does Iker take the deposit and pay you later?

## fact (EXPLANATION) - The fact

It does not. Clients pay by bank transfer straight to your own account. Iker never holds the money, and it takes no commission on bookings or deposits.

## how-it-works (DIAGRAM) - How it really works

What Iker does is keep the record. The client uploads proof of payment with the booking, and you check it and confirm it. Nothing counts as income until you confirm it.
