# cut: full (youtube-landscape, 120-180s)

## hook (PROBLEM) — The situation

> A phone at night: an Instagram DM thread. "How much for bridal makeup?", the reply "₦45,000", then "Last price, dear?". A second and third thread with the same question. All names fictional.

A message lands at ten at night. "How much for bridal makeup?" You reply: ₦45,000. Two minutes later: "Last price, dear?" Then someone else asks the same thing. By morning you have quoted three different prices, and you cannot remember who got which. So how do you stop haggling in your DMs without losing the client?

## gap (EXPLANATION) — Why the usual way breaks

> The price as a thought bubble over the owner, not written anywhere. Three chats, three different numbers.

Haggling starts because your price lives in your head, not where the client can see it. Every chat starts from zero, so the client tests the number. When you are tired, or a slot is empty, you drop it. The client who pushed hardest pays the least. The loyal one who never asked pays full price.

## how-it-works (DIAGRAM) — A better way

> Three steps: 1. Write every price down. 2. Show it before the chat. 3. Decide discounts in advance. The owner stays in charge of every number.

This fix works with or without software. Write down every service, its price and what it includes. Put that list where clients see it before they message you. And decide your discounts in advance, not in the middle of a chat. You still set every price. You just stop renegotiating it one DM at a time.

## product-intro (DEMO) — In Iker

> Dashboard sidebar, "Rate card" highlighted, then the services list. Point at price, duration, included and excluded items. Demo data, labelled.

In Iker, that list is your Rate card. Each service has a name, a price, a duration, and what is included and what is not. The business on screen is made up, with demo data.

## booking (DEMO) — On the booking link

> The public booking page on a phone: services with prices, then "Sort by price", then the slot picker with open times only.

Clients see it on your booking link, the page you put in your Instagram bio or WhatsApp status. Every service shows its price, and they can sort by price. Then they pick a time that is really open. The question changes from "how much?" to "which slot?"

## payment (DEMO) — The deposit, up front

> The slot step's header with the deposit and total split, then the payment details and proof of payment upload.

If you take a deposit, it is set on the service as a percentage of the price, so the client sees it while booking. They transfer straight to your account and upload proof of payment. Iker records it but never holds the money.

## confirm (EXPLANATION) — Discounts on your terms

> Sales & discounts: a promotion with its own link and a percentage off chosen services. Caption: "Depending on your plan".

Discounts are still yours to give. Depending on your plan, Sales and discounts creates a promotion with its own link and a percentage off the services you choose. Only people with that link get the offer, and the discount is recorded in your books.

## outcome (OUTCOME) — What changes

> Back to the DM: a new "Last price, dear?" and the reply is the booking link. No promises about bookings or income.

So the next time someone writes "last price, dear?", you send your booking link. Everyone sees the same price and what it includes, and any discount is one you planned. You answer with a link, not a negotiation.
